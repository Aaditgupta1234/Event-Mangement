## 📱 How to Use

### Step-by-Step Guide

1. **Go to Memory Reel** 📸
   - Click on "Memory Reel" in navigation

2. **Upload Photos**
   - Click "+ Add Memory"
   - Drag & drop or click to browse
   - Support: JPG, PNG, WebP (max 5MB)

3. **Create Your First Reel** 🎬
   - Click "🎬 Create Reel" button
   - Select minimum 2 photos
   - Enter a title (e.g., "Festival Highlights")

4. **Apply Effects** ✨
   - Choose your transition style
   - Adjust duration (how long each photo shows)
   - Pick a color filter
   - Select a music mood
   - Choose text appearance

5. **Preview** 👁️
   - See effects applied to first image
   - Check effects summary
   - Verify title appearance

6. **Create & Download** ⬇️
   - Click "Create Reel"
   - Video generates automatically
   - Download to your device
   - Share with friends!
